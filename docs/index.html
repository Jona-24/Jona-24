MASTER'S PROJECT / AI FOR DECISION SUPPORT

DataGovIA

How can an AI system turn organizational context and data-management knowledge into useful decisions? DataGovIA explores this through a knowledge-based decision support system for data governance.

Explore repository & architecture ↗
  1. Organizational context
  2. Symptoms & capability gaps
  3. Risks
  4. Recommendations
  5. Expected outcomes

01 / Structured domain knowledge

A Knowledge Model and Knowledge Units organize concepts from data governance, data architecture, and metadata management. The documented model draws on DAMA-DMBOK, DCAM, ISO 11179, and COBIT, translating domain knowledge into capabilities the system can reason about.

02 / Retrieval & agent workflows

Python, LangGraph, and Chroma support a modular architecture for intake, diagnosis, knowledge retrieval, reasoning, decision support, strategy, and technology assessment. Shared graph state carries organizational context, capability gaps, retrieved knowledge, and recommendations through the workflow.

03 / From diagnosis to action

The system aims to connect a capability gap with relevant knowledge, risks, and contextual recommendations. Its focus is the organization's capabilities and decisions, rather than simply returning framework definitions.

04 / Evaluation & limitations

This is a research prototype. My robustness evaluation identified limits in unsupported inference and counterfactual reasoning. Those findings make evaluation central to the work: useful recommendations need to remain grounded in the evidence and context available.

Developed through my master's-level work in Big Data and Analytics with an emphasis on AI, DataGovIA brings my governance experience into knowledge engineering and AI application design.
